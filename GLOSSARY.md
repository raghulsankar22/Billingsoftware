# Finance Billing

## Language

**Business account**:
A shop ledger with its own bills, numbering and cash book. It is distinct from a person's sign-in account.

**Delivery**:
The recorded return of pledged jewellery. A delivery calculation is not evidence that money was received.

**Collection**:
An explicitly confirmed customer payment, separating the principal and interest actually received from any interest discount.

**Disbursement**:
The money actually paid to a borrower. It may be less than the loan principal when a charge was withheld; the difference is not automatically treated as cash received.

**Cash book**:
A dated record of confirmed money received and paid, with Cash, UPI and Bank kept separate.

**Daily closing**:
A comparison of the expected physical cash balance with the amount actually counted for one business account and date.

**Reversal**:
A separate correction that offsets an earlier money record without erasing it.
